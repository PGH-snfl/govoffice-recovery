연합인증
연합인증 가입 기관의 연구자들은 소속기관의 인증정보(ID와 암호)를 이용해 다른 대학, 연구기관, 서비스 공급자의 다양한 온라인 자원과 연구 데이터를 이용할 수 있습니다.
이는 여행자가 자국에서 발행 받은 여권으로 세계 각국을 자유롭게 여행할 수 있는 것과 같습니다.
연합인증으로 이용이 가능한 서비스는 NTIS, DataON, Edison, Kafe, Webinar 등이 있습니다.
한번의 인증절차만으로 연합인증 가입 서비스에 추가 로그인 없이 이용이 가능합니다.
다만, 연합인증을 위해서는 최초 1회만 인증 절차가 필요합니다. (회원이 아닐 경우 회원 가입이 필요합니다.)
연합인증 절차는 다음과 같습니다.
최초이용시에는
ScienceON에 로그인 → 연합인증 서비스 접속 → 로그인 (본인 확인 또는 회원가입) → 서비스 이용
그 이후에는
ScienceON 로그인 → 연합인증 서비스 접속 → 서비스 이용
연합인증을 활용하시면 KISTI가 제공하는 다양한 서비스를 편리하게 이용하실 수 있습니다.

I. Necessity of Research
○ In the era of the fourth industrial revolution, the intelligent information age, intelligence information technology such as artificial intelligence (AI) and big data is expected to play a key role as a revolutionary intelligent revolution based on the digital technology.
○ The information protection industry is accelerating its development of artificial intelli gence-based information security solutions and is conducting intensive research anddevelopment investments in global security firms such as IBM and Oracle.
○ Need to develop and apply cyber threat semi-automatic analysis-based security control technologies to ensure rapid and accurate security controls and response to rapidlychanging cyber threats
○ Proactive system is needed in research and development in order to National Science and Science Technology Resource in response to a preemptive cyber-attack and leakprevention
Ⅱ. The Goal and Contents of Research
A. Final goals of research
․ Improved rapidity in response to cyber threats through automatic analysis and processing
․ A Study on the Application and Diffusion of Automatic Security Control Tech nology Using Artificial Intelligence
B. Contents of research
○ 24 hour x 365 day, Reinforce rapid and accurate cyberattacks through real-time security controls and analysis
○ The highest level of security control in Korea The combination of research and development technology and operation know-how maximizes the speed of cyber attack detection, analysis and response.
○ Research and application of large-scale network-based abnormal behavior detection, analysis and response technology
○ Preventive information protection activities to counter cyber threats
○ Strengthen preventive activities through diagnosis of vulnerable elements in researchers
○ Strengthening the security system of major information systems
Ⅱ. Conclusion and Application Plans
○ Improving the quality of all-periodic information protection services centered on the Science and Technology Cyber Security Center based on the security control paradigm shift (manual → semi-automatic → complete automation)
○ Developed artificial intelligence algorithm and next generation security control system to contribute to not only securing of intellectual property rights by spreading and spreading related fields but also upgrading level of national security control technology
○ Research in the science and technology field by enhancing the level of protection of the data ecosystem, expanding the research of large-scale challenges and challenges,and contributing to strengthening the national competitiveness of the science andtechnology sector
